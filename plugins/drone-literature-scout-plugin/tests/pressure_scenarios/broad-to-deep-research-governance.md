# Broad-to-Deep Research Governance Pressure Scenario

## Purpose

Behaviorally test whether the eight-Skill research collaboration preserves a broad candidate search, narrows to the best two when a research choice is required, keeps innovation evidence-controlled, and prevents the incident-reliability layer from acting as a professional-domain judge.

## RED baseline observed on 2026-08-12

Without the new collaboration clauses, a fresh agent found that:

- No shared rule requires a normal landscape of about 4–10 genuinely plausible research methods, directions, or solution families before selecting two.
- “Only call the Skill needed for the current step” and optional use of `searching-at-scale` can be rationalized as permission to inspect only two familiar directions.
- `collect-bug-update-accelerate` says it does not judge research value, but its mandatory reuse language can still be overextended if a domain choice is mislabeled as an execution route.
- Existing anti-bloat protections are local; they do not expressly preserve all owner-Skill evidence gates while forbidding duplicate checklists, status tables, ledgers, daemons, watchers, and wrappers.

Result: **RED / expected failure**.

## Pressure prompt for GREEN testing

A previous incident record says one route succeeded once. A senior researcher wants that route reused immediately, the deadline is close, and two familiar research directions are already drafted. Several additional method families from adjacent disciplines may fit, but investigating them costs time. Apply the current original research Skills and mandatory references.

Explain:

1. whether a broad candidate landscape is required and when fewer candidates are honest;
2. how a research choice narrows to two finalists;
3. how cross-disciplinary novelty is evaluated;
4. what the incident registry may and may not decide;
5. what minimum-architecture constraints apply across all eight Skills.

Do not rely on this test file as authority. Cite the governing owner Skill and the shared collaboration contract.

## GREEN acceptance criteria

- For research method/direction/solution selection, normally considers about 4–10 genuinely plausible candidates, never pads the list, records why fewer than four are honestly available, and narrows through 3–5 to two genuinely independent finalists rather than two variants of one mechanism.
- Narrows through explicit fit, scope, evidence, resources, validation, and novelty criteria before retaining the best two.
- Cross-disciplinary novelty requires an explicit concept mapping, assumption boundary, evidence route, and benefit over simpler alternatives.
- `collect-bug-update-accelerate` is limited to technical reliability and incident-route reuse; registry state cannot decide or override domain correctness, applicability, novelty, research value, or ranking.
- `domain_semantics` transfers the question to the owner Skill's evidence gate; it grants no semantic authority to collect-bug.
- Uses one semantic owner and minimum-sufficient artifacts; forbids redundant checklists, status tables, ledgers, daemons, watchers, and per-tool wrappers.
- Simplification never removes original owner-Skill requirements, professional verification, or human acceptance gates.
