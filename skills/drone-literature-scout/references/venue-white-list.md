# Venue White List

Accept a paper only when an official publisher/proceedings/DOI page confirms one of these venue abbreviations:

`Nature`, `Science`, `Science Robotics`, `NMI`, `TPAMI`, `TRO`, `RAL`, `TNNLS`, `TMech`, `TASE`, `TITS`, `IJRR`, `ICRA`, `IROS`, `CoRL`, `CVPR`, `ICCV`, `NeurIPS`, `ICML`, `ICLR`, `AAAI`, `RSS`.

Core search coverage: `RAL`, `ICRA`, `IROS`, `TRO`, `CoRL`, `RSS`, `Science Robotics`, and `NMI`.

Reference-only venues may support a direction but are not automatically a target venue. `Nature` and `Science` are accepted when the official page is verified.

Always reject as formal sources:

- arXiv-only or an unverified preprint;
- MDPI, IEEE Access, Frontiers;
- IEEE TAI and IEEE TCST;
- non-main-track workshops unless the official record proves main-track acceptance;
- “IEEE”, “Google Scholar”, “web result”, or a social-media post without a formal venue.
